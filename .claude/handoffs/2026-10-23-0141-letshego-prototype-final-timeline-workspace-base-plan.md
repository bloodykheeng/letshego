## Decisions settled this session (with the user's reasons)
- **The user's notes are the reference**; build nothing beyond them ("don't over-exaggerate", said twice).
- **Hierarchy is Branch › Territory › Route**, three levels; they are **labels that filter** customers, lists and
  maps ("those are just filters"). Rejected: regions, regional manager, drawn areas.
- **Prospect** = name, phone, location. **Lead** = + NIN, loan amount, location. Rejected: "where you met", notes,
  "what for", best day to visit.
- **No calling in the app** ("this is not a dialer app"). Rejected: call screens, call buttons, calls made.
- **"KYC completed"**, not "KYC captured" ("feels weird"). **Client = loan disbursed** (team: "clients are when a
  loan is disbursed"). Rejected: KYC completed = client (my assumption).
- **Prospect targets**, its own section: a route and a target per agent per day (user chose the name: "this is
  like prospect targets"). **Journey plans** = lead journey plans for officers. Rejected: "route journey plan".
- **Geofence as in NICE** (`VisitProximityService` + NICE's user manual): check-in within **50 m of the lead's
  saved GPS location**, refused otherwise, no location blocks check-in, check-out never blocked, distance stored.
  Rejected: territory boundaries and "left the territory" alerts.
- **One role per user**; extra work is *assigned* (Joel gets prospect targets for a day). Rejected: two roles.
- **Navigation:** four-tab bottom bar, actions on their screens, summary cards not clickable, stat cards open their
  lists, plans open from a list first, Back returns to where you came from.
- **Console menu:** Sales (Overview) · Planning (Prospect targets, Journey plans) · Field · Loans · Admin.
- **Build approach:** copy GiftNest's foundation (it is the newest; GiftNest copied SDS's the same way); use
  Alpha's page-access model, no self sign-up, Larastan level 5 with no baseline. Folders named like GiftNest;
  branch `farouk`.
